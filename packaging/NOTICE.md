# Oni (oni-rs) — notices

A preservation port of Bungie's Oni (2001). It contains none of the
original game: the player brings their own copy of the Anniversary
Edition, which the game converts on first start.

- The engine and tools (oni-rs): MIT OR Apache-2.0.
- `oni-bink` (Bink 1 video decoding, from ra2-engine): GPL-3.0-or-later.
  A build that includes it is distributed under the GPL-3.0; its source
  is the oni-rs repository.
- The launcher's font, Rajdhani SemiBold (Indian Type Foundry): SIL Open
  Font License 1.1 (`Rajdhani-OFL.txt`).
- Oni is a trademark of its owners; this project is not affiliated with
  them.
