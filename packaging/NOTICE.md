# Oni (oni-rs) — notices

A preservation port of Bungie's Oni (2001). It contains none of the
original game: the player brings their own copy of the Anniversary
Edition, which the game converts on first start.

- oni-rs — the engine, the converter and their crates: GPL-3.0-or-later
  (`LICENSE`). Anyone may use, change and share it; what they share
  stays under the GPL with its source.
- Bink 1's constant tables (`oni-bink/src/tables.rs`) come from FFmpeg's
  `libavcodec/binkdata.h`: LGPL-2.1-or-later (taken under the GPL here).
- The launcher's font, Rajdhani SemiBold (Indian Type Foundry): SIL Open
  Font License 1.1 (`Rajdhani-OFL.txt`).
